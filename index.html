Sudoku Alliances Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Introduction

Sudoku Alliances is a seasonal, symbol-based puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay information locally on your device. This may include your current level, coins, unlocked rewards, selected cosmetics and game preferences.

3. Personal information

The current version does not intentionally collect personal information through in-game accounts, advertising or analytics features.

4. Advertising and analytics

The current version does not include integrated advertising or analytics services.

5. Sharing information

The current version does not include an online account system or online leaderboard service that we have identified as sharing player information. Your device operating system and third-party services may process information independently.

6. Children's privacy

We do not intentionally collect children's personal information through the game's current features.

7. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by the game, removed through available device settings, or erased when the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this webpage.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email support.sudokualliances@gmail.com.
